# Turnos

## Planillas are opened by hose and closed with final readings
Since 2026-10-21 a planilla is created only with ABRIR TURNO (TurnoController@abrir, ManguerasTurnoService), never by Guardar. To open, the islero picks fecha (today or tomorrow; the night turno is turno 1 of the NEXT day; the administrador can pick any date), turno_dia, hora_inicio and the hoses (TurnoSurtidor::MANGUERAS) he will work. Isleros never share hoses: a hose can be in only one turnos.estado = 'abierta' planilla. The initial reading of each hose is the last final reading of a CLOSED planilla (ManguerasTurnoService::ultimasLecturas), never the reading of a turno that is still open. Turno 29 of 2026-10-20 got zeros because the previous turno was still being filled. On save, the server keeps the stored initial reading and ignores hoses that are not part of the turno. CERRAR TURNO requires every final reading and none may be below its initial; a blank final reading is null (pending), not 0. The jefe de patios can close an islero's open planilla with final readings only (turnos.cerrar). Revisar requires a closed planilla. Correcting a final reading that the next turno using that hose already took as its initial (phase 2): the first save fails with a 'correccion' error that lists the change. Saving again with confirmar_correccion=1 also updates that turno's initial reading and recalculates its gallons, value and sobrante_pendiente. If that turno is revisado, only the administrador can do it, and the turno goes back to pending review. Counter change (phase 3, administrador/gerente only, TurnoController@cambioContador): turno_surtidores.contador_retiro and contador_instalacion. Gallons = (retiro - inicial) + (final - instalación), computed in TurnoSurtidor::calcularGalones(). Every path that computes gallons must use that function. Both actions are logged in lecturas_correcciones.
